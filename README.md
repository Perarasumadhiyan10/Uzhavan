## 📖 About the Project

**Uzhavan** is a modern agricultural marketplace that connects farmers directly with buyers, eliminating middlemen and ensuring transparent grain trading.

Farmers can upload available grain stock with images, quantity, price, and location. Buyers can browse available stock and reserve the exact quantity they need.

A unique feature of the system is the **24-hour booking system**. If the buyer does not collect the grains within 24 hours, the booking is automatically cancelled and the stock becomes available again.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. Farmer logs into the system.
2. Farmer uploads grain details.
3. Buyer browses available grains.
4. Buyer books required quantity.
5. System reserves stock.
6. 24-hour timer starts.
7. Buyer collects the grain.
8. Order completed.
9. If not collected within 24 hours, booking is automatically cancelled.
